Your 90-day LinkedIn roadmap

Here's how to think about the next 3 months — broken into focused phases so momentum doesn't fade after the cohort ends.

Now — Week 1–2
Lock in the routine
Your Sunday batch system is your most important habit. Protect it. Don't add anything new — just run the system you built in Week 9 consistently.
Weeks 3–4
Deepen your niche voice
Look back at your best 2–3 posts. What made them land? Double down on that angle, format, or topic. You are finding your signature style.
Month 2 (Weeks 5–8)
Activate a big swing
Launch your newsletter, host an event, or write a long-form piece. One bold move per month compounds over time. Choose your Month 2 big swing now.
Month 3 (Weeks 9–12)
Harvest and review
Audit your analytics. Which topics drove the most connections, DMs, or opportunities? Set your next 90-day goals based on what you now know works for you.
Day 90
Reflect and reset
Look back at who you were on May 14. Write a LinkedIn post about your 90-day journey. That post will be some of the most powerful content you've ever shared.

Closing reflection — 5 questions that matter

Take 5–10 minutes with these. Your answers are just for you — but they will shape your next chapter.

1. What did you discover about yourself through this process?
Think about: a moment you almost didn't post but did. What happened?
2. What belief about yourself shifted in these 9 weeks?
Start with: "I used to believe... Now I know..."
3. What does "influence" mean to you now vs. when you started?
There are no wrong answers — your definition is yours.
4. Who in your community needs to see you show up more visibly?
Think beyond your current network — who could you reach in the next 90 days?
5. What is the one thing you will NOT go back to?
This is your commitment. Say it out loud if you can.
